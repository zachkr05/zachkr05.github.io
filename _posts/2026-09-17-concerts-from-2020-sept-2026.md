So far I have seen Playboi Carti, Ken Carson, Destroy Lonely, Yung Lean, Bladee, Death Grips, Machine Girl, Show Me the Body, and the Taxidermists.

Currently, I am super into EDM, punk, and emo. My favourite group is without a doubt gingerbee, but they broke up :(.

Recently, I have been listening to a lot of Gil Scott-Heron, Marvin Gaye, The Garden, and Weatherday.
