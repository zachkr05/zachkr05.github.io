An ongoing collection of **full implementations of reinforcement learning algorithms** and written exercise responses from *Reinforcement Learning: An Introduction* by **Richard S. Sutton and Andrew G. Barto**.

I am working through the book chapter by chapter, implementing the algorithms in Python and recording the ideas behind them. The collection currently includes a Chapter 2 bandit experiment and Chapter 3 exercise notes.
