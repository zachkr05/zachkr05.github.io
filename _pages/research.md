## NSF GRFP Research Proposal

### ContinualToM: Learning and Planning with Evolving Models of Other Minds

*Enabling Robots to Anticipate and Adapt to Human Goals, Beliefs, and Intentions*

Research proposal prepared for the **NSF Graduate Research Fellowship Program (GRFP)**.

How can a robot recognize when its understanding of a collaborator is incomplete? This proposal investigates agents that detect when their current models cannot explain a partner's behavior, learn new representations of goals and beliefs through interaction, and plan using those evolving models.

The proposed evaluation examines adaptation to unfamiliar partner goals and beliefs, retention of earlier knowledge, and improvements in task completion and coordination in collaborative environments.
