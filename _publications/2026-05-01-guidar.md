When a person reaches into a robot's plan and drags its trajectory somewhere else, that edit carries information about an objective the robot cannot observe directly. GuidAR treats each edit as supervision rather than as a one-off override: a mixture-of-experts conditional diffusion model learns costmaps from human trajectory corrections made in an augmented-reality interface, using value-function-derived supervision so that the correction shapes the robot's *future* plans as well as its current one.

The system was built and evaluated end to end — a Unity AR client on the human side, a diffusion-model backend, Vicon motion capture, and a ROSbot ground vehicle, tied together by a 100 Hz TCP pipeline.
